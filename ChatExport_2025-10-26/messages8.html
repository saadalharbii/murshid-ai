Safety Guidance re. Vile Hate Letter to Hurt Muslims*

Assalamu Alaikum Wa Rahmatullah

The MCB is aware of the threatening letters that have been sent to Muslim families and businesses across Bradford, Leicester, Sheffield, Cardiff and London, calling on members of the British public to “punish” Muslims through a series of vile points-based actions.

We share the deep distress and alarm these letters have caused. Sadly, it is reflective of an increasing hatred against Muslims which manifests itself alongside the rise of the far right. We have called on elected officials to stand up and take action against Islamophobia. No bigotry of this kind should be tolerated, accepted or promoted in society.

North East counter terror police are investigating this coordinated attempt to incite violence and strike fear into the heart of our communities. If you have received a copy of the letter, please:
- *Report it* immediately to your local police force, or
- *Call 101* and quote incident number 44 of 9th March
- *Don’t* handle the letter any more than necessary
- *Place it* in an envelope or plastic bag for later examination

We urge everyone to remain vigilant and alert, but calm during this time. Take sensible precautions to keep your homes, businesses, mosques and community centres safe. Please avoid using the date on the ‘Punish a Muslim’ letter publicly, so as not to give it any undue prominence.

- If you have CCTV installed, please ensure it’s working
- Take some time to register possible risks at your mosque or place of work
- Have volunteers ready at prayer or busy times for emergencies
- If you know anyone who has received the letter, please reach out and see if they need any support. In times of distress, a support network is key
