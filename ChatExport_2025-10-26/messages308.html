Just to remind again following Health Tips in Ramadan !
1. Do not Compromise on Sleep : Maintain 8 hours of Sleep especially in due consideration of late night wakfulness and early Sahour. It make you foucus and concentrate at work.

2. Keep Your Hydration – Consider using good amount of water, especially when we have a breif period of allowance to eat, from Evening to Sahour. A target of 2 liters is a perfect goal.

3. Eat healthy and Nutritious Foods : Eat food of good choices with a mix of soups, fruits, vegetables and of course special choices of Ramadan. Avoid too much of sweat. Try to eat in breaks with small quantities. Avoid process foods by maximum.

4. Do More Work Early in Morning : Try schedule work with high in physical demand or mental efforts in early part of day.

5. Do NOT Stop Exercising – Although you may feel more tired and understandably less active while fasting, skipping regular exercise for a full month is unhealthy. One of best time may be few hours before fasting.
Same time don't push too hard toward doing physical.

6. Be Mindful for COVID-19 Risk & Exercise Precautions: We fast and share food in Ramadan, but same time please be mindful for gatherings. A good point always to remeber, be vigilant for people who are sick, avoid crowded gathering, inviting one family one time will probably give much space to meet.
This is an exceptional year, where we are heading towards normalisation of life with COVID-19, and with vaccination.
Stay Blessed in this Holy month of Ramadan.
